SL-001 replaces cumulative token enforcement with the explicit context_tokens ceiling in a new policy version. Preserve cumulative token totals for reporting and accounting; do not retain an optional cumulative token enforcement control in the new policy. Request count, cost and wall-clock ceilings remain independent existing controls. Callers may lower context_tokens through per-call ceilings, bounded by the operator policy maximum. This replaces the cumulative proxy that prematurely stops many-turn runs. Existing-policy and caller migration is the separate inq-4 decision.
